# Jobnex - Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Overview
Jobnex is an AI-powered Resume Analyzer application designed to help job seekers evaluate and optimize their resumes against job descriptions.

## Key Features
- **Resume Analysis**: Extract skills, calculate match scores, and provide actionable improvement recommendations.
- **Privacy & Redaction**: Automatically redact personal contact info (email, phone, URLs) before storing or sharing resumes.
- **Analysis History**: Store past analyses in MySQL and retrieve them with a single click.
